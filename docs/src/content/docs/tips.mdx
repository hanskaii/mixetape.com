## Keep the media in mixetape storage

A post is sent to the platform only at its lead time — which can be days after you schedule
it. Whatever `mediaUrl` points at has to still be there then. Links from render services,
signed download URLs and temporary hosts often expire before that; some hosts also refuse or
throttle the platforms' fetchers.

Files in mixetape storage stay put for as long as a post needs them:

- **From your machine:** `create_upload` gives a presigned URL; send the file in one PUT
  (up to 5 GB), call `finish_upload`, and schedule the `r2://` URL. The file never passes
  through the agent's context.
- **From a link that will expire:** `import_file` copies it into storage right away.

See [Media and storage](/concepts/media).

## Schedule ahead, even for "now"

A scheduled post waits in mixetape — editable and cancellable — until shortly before its time,
while a post without `scheduledAt` goes straight out. When there is any chance of a
correction, give it a time a little ahead: you keep the chance to fix it.

## Keep the default lead on YouTube

The 30 minutes before go-live is when YouTube processes the HD versions. With `leadMinutes: 0`
a long video can go public while it is still low resolution. See
[Posts and timing](/concepts/posts-and-timing).

## Always write times with an offset

`2026-10-02T17:30:00+07:00`, never `2026-10-02T17:30:00` — a time without an offset is read as
UTC, which for Jakarta is seven hours late.

## Put the extras on the post

`thumbnailUrl`, `playlistIds`, `captions` and `firstComment` in the post's `metadata` are
applied automatically right after the upload — no second call to remember, no window where
the video is live without them. `set_thumbnail`, `add_to_collection` and `upload_caption` are
for posts that are already up.

A `firstComment` on a Short is a good place for a link to the full video.

## Check how it went

After a post's time, `get_post` tells you how it went: `published` with its link, or `failed`
with the reason. Fix what the reason says and `retry_post` — it goes now, or at its original
time if that is still ahead. YouTube's upload quota resets at midnight Pacific time; retry a
post that failed on it after that.

## Make a pipeline safe to run twice

A script that may be retried should look before it schedules: `list_posts` with `from`/`to`
around the time, and skip the post if one with the same `mediaUrl` is already there. Then an
interrupted run never schedules the same video twice.

## One key per agent

Give each agent or script its own [API key](/concepts/api-keys) with only the permissions it
needs. A pipeline usually needs `read`, `publish` and `storage`; only an agent you trust to
add channels needs `channels`. Removing one key then never stops another.

## Watch for `reconnect`

A channel whose access was revoked — or that mixetape needs new permissions from — shows
`status: "reconnect"` in `list_accounts`, and its posts fail until it is connected again.
The agent can hand you a fresh sign-in link with `connect_channel`.
