**Posting:** a video (up to 5 minutes), a JPEG or PNG image, or several files (`media`) as a
carousel of 2–20. Threads cannot schedule through its API, so mixetape prepares the post
`leadMinutes` early (10 by default) and publishes it at `scheduledAt`.

## Metadata

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `text` | At most 500 characters; defaults to the post caption |
| `firstComment` | Posted as the profile's own reply once the post is public |

## After it is up

Replies work like comments: read them, reply as the profile, and hide them. Post insights
cover views, likes, replies, reposts, quotes and shares.

## Limits

- 250 posts per profile in 24 hours.
