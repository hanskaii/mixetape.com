**Connect:** any Bluesky account. Signing in uses Bluesky's own page (AT Protocol OAuth);
give your handle (`you.bsky.social`) to go straight to your account's server and have it
filled in — it is optional for accounts hosted by Bluesky. Agents pass it as `account` to
`connect_channel`.

**Posting:** a video (up to 3 minutes and 100 MB), an image, or 2–4 images. Bluesky cannot
hold a post until a time, so mixetape publishes it at `scheduledAt`. Images over 1 MB, Bluesky's
limit, are made smaller on the way when they are in mixetape storage. Links, `#hashtags` and
`@handles` in the text become links.

## Metadata

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `text` | At most 300 characters; defaults to the post caption |
| `langs` | Up to 3 language codes, e.g. `["en"]` |
| `altTexts` | Descriptions of the images or video, one per file, in order |
| `contentWarning` | `sexual`, `nudity`, `porn` or `graphic-media` — labels the media |
| `firstComment` | Posted as the account's own reply once the post is up — a thread |

## After it is up

Replies work like comments: read them and reply as the account. Status shows the post's likes
and replies. Bluesky has no analytics API.

## Limits

- Hiding replies is not supported yet.
- A retried publish never posts twice: each attempt writes one record with a fixed key.
