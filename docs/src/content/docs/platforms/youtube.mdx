**Connect:** sign in with the Google account that owns the channel. Every channel it manages
can be connected.

**Posting:** uploaded as private `leadMinutes` early (30 by default), with a publish time;
YouTube makes it public at `scheduledAt`. A vertical video under three minutes becomes a Short.

## Metadata

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| `title` | No `<` or `>` |
| `description` | No `<` or `>` |
| `category` | Category id, e.g. `"27"` Education, `"22"` People |
| `tags` | At most 500 characters in total |
| `privacyStatus` | `public`, `unlisted` or `private` |
| `madeForKids` | `true` / `false` |
| `notifySubscribers` | `true` / `false` |
| `defaultLanguage` | Language of the title and description, e.g. `"en"`; needed for `localizations` |
| `localizations` | Translations: `{ "id": { "title": "…", "description": "…" } }` |
| `thumbnailUrl` | Public https JPEG/PNG, at most 2 MB, 1280×720; set right after upload |
| `playlistIds` | Playlists (`list_collections`) the video joins right after upload |
| `captions` | Subtitle files uploaded right after the video |
| `firstComment` | Posted as the channel's own comment once the video is public |

## After it is up

Change its details (`edit_published_post`), set a thumbnail, manage playlists and captions,
read and moderate comments, and read analytics — views, watch time, audience retention and
traffic sources, per video and per channel.

## Limits

- **Quota:** per day, 100 uploads plus 10,000 units for everything else (a thumbnail or an
  edit costs 50, a caption 400). mixetape reports a used-up quota as a failed post with the
  reason; retry after it resets at midnight Pacific time.
- **Custom thumbnails** need a verified channel.
- The API cannot pin comments or set a Short's related video.
- Analytics lag two to three days.
